How you pay the monthly fee: with PayPal

You pay each month in advance. A few days before, you get a PayPal invoice by email with the amount and your reference. We remind you. 5 days after the due date, access is paused. Your data is kept for 90 days. Everything comes back once your payment is confirmed.
