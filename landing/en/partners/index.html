FAQ

Partner questions

How much do I earn?

20% of what each customer you refer pays during their first 12 months. For example, a service company with 5 technicians pays $145 a month, so you would earn $29 for each month they pay, for up to 12 months.

When do I get paid?

Monthly, once the customer you referred pays their invoice. Nothing is owed for free trial days or for invoices that are not paid.

Does it cost anything to join? Is there a minimum?

No. Joining is free and there is no minimum number of referrals.

Do my clients pay more because they came through me?

No. They get the normal prices ($29 per technician per month for service crews, $19 per store per month for shops) and the normal 7-day free trial.
