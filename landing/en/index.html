FAQ

What people ask us before they start

Getting started

Which of the two apps do I need?

If you have technicians who go to the customer, it's Services. If you sell products and take orders, it's Store. Not sure? .

Is the app in English?

Yes. You, your technicians and your customers use it in English, and each person can switch it to Spanish on their own phone. Your Store can be in English or Spanish, with prices in dollars.

Do I have to install anything?

No. Open it in the browser on your phone or computer. If you want, add a shortcut to your home screen so it opens like an app. It does need an internet connection.

Do my technicians need a special phone?

No. The phone they already have works, Android or iPhone, with internet. They take photos with the same phone's camera.

Will my technicians know how to use it?

When they sign in they see their jobs, with buttons to call the customer, open the map, "Start" and "Complete". The best way to know is to try it for 7 days with your crew.

How long does it take to get started?

Fill out the form and the account is created on its own. Your login usually arrives by email within minutes, with a temporary password. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder or email us at contacto@novaworkorder.com.

Is this for watching my technicians all day?

No. Location is off by default. If you turn it on and the technician agrees, it's saved only when they tap "Start" and "Complete". If you turn on the live map, you see them only while they have a job in progress and the app open. The technician can remove the permission anytime. Your customers never see their location.

Pricing and payments

How much does it cost? Is there a contract?

Services: $29 per technician a month. Store: $19 per store a month. The PayPal invoice is for that amount. No contract and no fees.

What happens after the 7 days?

Nothing is charged automatically, because we don't ask for a card. A few days before, you get the PayPal invoice for the first month by email. If you pay it, you keep everything you set up. If not, access is paused and your data is kept for 90 days.

Do you charge a commission per sale?

No. With Store you pay the monthly fee and that's it. Your customers pay you directly: on delivery or with your own Stripe, PayPal or Square payment link. If you use a payment link, that company charges its own fee.

How do I pay the monthly fee?

With PayPal, month to month, in advance. You get a PayPal invoice by email with the amount and your reference. Pay it with your PayPal account or with any credit or debit card, no PayPal account needed. We don't store card details.

What if I miss a month?

We remind you. 5 days after the due date, access is paused. With Store, your shop also stops taking orders. Your data isn't deleted: it's kept for 90 days. Everything comes back once your payment is confirmed.

Can I cancel? Do I get a refund?

Cancel anytime: let us know before your next invoice and you keep access until the end of the month you paid for. Months already started aren't refunded, except where the law requires it. For 90 days you can ask us for a copy of your data.

With Services, how many technicians do I pay for? Can I change it?

You pay for the technicians you sign up for. That's the maximum number of active technicians on your account, and it's charged in full even if you use fewer that month. Your admins and your customers don't pay. To change the number, write to us; the charge changes starting the next month.

Can I charge my customers from the app?

With Services, the quote is in dollars and your customer approves it online, but you collect the payment your own way: the app doesn't take card payments or make invoices. With Store, your customer pays you on delivery or with your own payment link.

What it doesn't do yet

What Services doesn't do yet
  • It doesn't work offline and it isn't in the app stores: it opens in the browser.
  • It doesn't send automatic messages to your customers: you send them the link from your WhatsApp with one tap, or copy it and text it.
  • It doesn't take card payments from your customers or make invoices.
  • It has no calendar, scheduled maintenance plans, parts inventory or timesheets.
  • It doesn't import customers from Excel: you add them in the app.
What Store doesn't do yet
  • It doesn't take online payments on its own: your customer pays on delivery or with your own payment link.
  • Automatic WhatsApp messages require connecting your own number to Meta's platform, and Meta charges for those messages. There are no email notifications.
  • It doesn't charge cards at the counter: you record the payment you took on your own terminal.
  • Your store lives at pedidos.novaworkorder.com/your-shop, with up to 2,000 products.
  • The drivers are yours: NOVA doesn't provide drivers.

For those who want more detail: users, history and other systems

Services has three kinds of user: admin, technician and customer. Technicians see, create and edit the jobs, customers and quotes of the whole business; only admins can delete. Customers see only their own. If you deactivate a user, they lose access right away. "Activity" shows who did what and when. You can require the customer's signature to close a job. It works in English or Spanish. For developers, there's an API and webhooks for jobs.

With Store, your employees handle orders, the register and products; only you see the settings and reports. It also has register closing, a barcode scanner, sales reports for Excel and a button to pause your store.
