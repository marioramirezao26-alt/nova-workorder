Privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026. It covers the chat on this page, the Meta pixel and NOVAWORKORDER Store.

NOVAWORKORDER is offered by NOVA Desarrollos ("we", "us"). This policy explains what personal data we collect on novaworkorder.com/en/ and in our apps (app.novaworkorder.com and pedidos.novaworkorder.com), what we use it for, who we share it with and the choices you have. NOVA Desarrollos is based in Colombia, so we also follow Colombian data protection law (Law 1581 of 2012). The full policy in Spanish covers all of our products in detail; if the two versions differ, that one prevails.

NOVA Desarrollos, which offers its apps under the NOVAWORKORDER brand. For anything related to your data: contacto@novaworkorder.com.

3. What we use it for

  • To answer your request, create your account and provide the service.
  • To let each business manage its jobs, customers and technicians, and let each store receive and fill its shoppers' orders.
  • To email you NOVAWORKORDER information related to your request or your account (the welcome email, help during the trial, a notice when it ends). Every email explains how to stop receiving them.
  • To send invoices and payment reminders and, if an invoice isn't paid, pause the account as described in the terms.
  • To give support, tell you about important changes and keep the service secure.
  • To meet legal, accounting and tax obligations.

We don't sell or rent personal data, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

4. Your customers' and shoppers' data

The data a business records about its own customers and workers, and the data shoppers leave when ordering from a Store shop, belongs to that business. The business decides how it's used, and we process it only to provide the service, following its instructions (as a "service provider" or "processor"). If you bought from a shop, that shop is responsible for your data; you pay the shop directly (on delivery or with its payment link) and NOVAWORKORDER never sees your money or your cards. Requests about that data go to the business first; if they reach us, we pass them on.

5. The chat on this page

In the chat you're answered by GABY, NOVAWORKORDER's virtual assistant with artificial intelligence, and the NOVA Desarrollos team reviews these conversations and may answer you in the same chat or, if you give us your email, by email.

  • What data: what you write with its date and time, and any details you give us (name, email, phone, business, city, size of your business), along with the page or campaign you came from. Your browser keeps a session code so the conversation continues if you reload the page. We don't store your IP address: only a code derived from it that can't be turned back into it, to limit abuse of the chat.
  • What for: to answer you, show you the app, help you start your free trial and follow up on sales.
  • Who processes it: to answer you, the text is processed by artificial intelligence model providers, through OpenRouter, which may be in the United States or other countries.
  • How long: the conversation is kept with your sales record while we have a business relationship or until you ask us to delete it.

Don't write passwords, payment details or sensitive information in the chat. If you prefer WhatsApp, a person from the team answers from the WhatsApp Business app; those messages travel through WhatsApp (Meta).

6. The Meta pixel

When you visit the page we ask whether you allow the Meta pixel. Only if you accept, the page loads Meta's code, which sets its own cookies in your browser and tells Meta (Facebook and Instagram) that you visited the page and, if it applies, that you started your free trial, asked for information or wrote to GABY, along with the country of the page (United States). We don't send Meta your name, email, phone or what you write. We use it to learn which ads work. If you don't accept, the page works the same. To change your mind, clear this site's data in your browser and the page will ask you again.

7. Who we share it with

Only with the providers we need to operate, under confidentiality: our server provider (Vultr), our domain provider (GoDaddy), PayPal for payments, the email and messaging services we use to contact you (Brevo, WhatsApp), OpenRouter and the AI providers behind the chat, and Meta, only if you accept the pixel. Some of them are in the United States or other countries. We'll also hand over data to an authority that legally requests it.

8. How long we keep it

  • Form requests: up to 2 years, or sooner if you ask us to delete them.
  • Account data (including its shoppers' orders): while the subscription is active. If it's canceled or paused for non-payment, we keep it for 90 days so you can come back or ask for a copy; after that we delete it. Daily backups delete themselves after 14 days.
  • Billing records: as long as accounting and tax law requires.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, ask how we use it or withdraw your consent, by writing to contacto@novaworkorder.com. We answer within 10 business days at most. We don't discriminate against anyone for using these rights. If you live in a US state with its own privacy law (for example, California), you have the rights that law gives you, and you can use the same email to exercise them.

10. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), encrypted passwords, isolation of each business's data, limits on login attempts, role-based permissions and daily backups. No system is perfect: if an incident affects your data, we'll tell you and report it to the authorities when required.

11. Children

Our services are for businesses and aren't directed to children under 13 (or to minors in general).

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with its date and, if the change is important, we'll let the users of our apps know.
