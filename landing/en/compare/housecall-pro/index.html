The short answer

Who each tool fits

NOVAWORKORDER fits you if…

  • You run a small crew, roughly 1 to 10 technicians.
  • You mostly need to stop losing jobs in texts, calls and paper.
  • You handle invoices and payments another way today.
  • You want a per-technician price, month to month, with office admins included.

Housecall Pro fits you better if…

  • You want online booking, estimates, invoicing and payments in one place.
  • You offer consumer financing, service plans or sales proposals.
  • You need GPS tracking, routes or route optimization.
  • You need QuickBooks Online sync, an open API or review management.

FAQ

Questions about switching

Is NOVAWORKORDER a full replacement for Housecall Pro?

Not for every business. Housecall Pro includes much more: online booking, estimates, invoicing, payments and consumer financing, review management, and on higher plans GPS tracking, routes, QuickBooks Online sync and an open API. NOVAWORKORDER focuses on getting work orders to your technicians' phones, with quotes your customer approves online and service reports with photos and the customer's signature. If you need invoicing, payments and booking in one tool, Housecall Pro is the better fit.

How much does NOVAWORKORDER cost compared with Housecall Pro?

NOVAWORKORDER is $29 per technician per month, month to month, with office admins included. For example, 5 technicians cost $145 a month. As of October 2026, Housecall Pro's pricing page lists Essentials (5 users included) at $189/mo billed monthly or $149/mo billed annually, and also shows introductory offers. Check Housecall Pro's pricing page for current numbers.

Do my technicians need to install an app?

No. NOVAWORKORDER runs in the browser of any phone (Android or iPhone) and computer. Technicians can add it to their home screen so it opens like an app.

Is there a contract or a credit card for the trial?

No. The 7-day free trial needs no credit card, and after that you pay month to month with no contract and no setup fee. You can cancel anytime.
